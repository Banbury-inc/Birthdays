Birthdays helps you remember and celebrate the people who matter.

Sign in with your phone number, sync your contacts, and instantly see which of your friends have birthdays coming up. No accounts to create, no passwords to remember.

FEATURES

- Quick phone-number sign-in with SMS verification
- Sync your contacts to match friends already using Birthdays
- See upcoming birthdays at a glance
- Add your own birthday so friends never miss it

Your contacts are only used to match birthdays with your friends and are never shared or sold.
